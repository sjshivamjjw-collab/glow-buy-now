--- LONG DESCRIPTION (≤ 4000 characters) ---

Ripple is where everyday moments become stories worth sharing.

A social app built in India for people who care about real recommendations from real people. Travel diaries, food finds, lifestyle picks, honest reviews and the little moments from your day that you actually want to remember — Ripple is the home for all of it.

WHAT YOU CAN DO ON RIPPLE

• Share photos, videos and short text posts in seconds
• Follow creators whose taste you trust
• Like, comment, save and share posts you love
• Join communities built around your interests — food, travel, books, fitness, art, and more
• Chat with members inside community rooms, RSVP to events, and download resources
• Get notified when someone replies, follows you, or mentions you
• Discover new creators and trending posts in a clean, distraction-free feed

WHY PEOPLE LOVE RIPPLE

No ads. No algorithmic noise. No influencer fakery.

Just real moments from real Ripplers — a small, growing community of people who want a better corner of the internet. We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and we don't manipulate your feed to keep you doom-scrolling.

BUILT FOR TRUST AND SAFETY

We take community safety seriously:
• Report any post, comment or profile with one tap
• Block users you don't want to interact with — they can never see or contact you again
• Delete your account anytime from inside the app — all your data is permanently removed
• Phone-based OTP login — no passwords to remember or leak
• All data encrypted in transit, full Privacy Policy at myripple.co.in/privacy

COMMUNITIES MADE SIMPLE

Creators can build their own communities with multiple membership tiers. Members get access to private chat channels, exclusive events, and curated resources — all inside the app, with no extra logins.

A SMALL TEAM IN INDIA

Ripple is built by a small team in Mumbai who genuinely care about the people who use it. We read every support email and respond within one business day.

Download Ripple and start sharing the little moments that make your day.

Need help? Visit myripple.co.in/support
